The fleet manager's week, left to right, with every story underneath the moment it belongs to. Rows are releases. Each card opens the requirement it stands for in the repository.

How to read the gaps

An empty cell in the later row is not an oversight, it means that activity is finished in v1 and nothing about it is deferred. An empty cell in v1.1 means the same. The only thing pulled out of the first release is the fleet roll-up, and that decision is J-24, with the condition that would reopen it written down beside it.

Column 1 carries no stories because nothing in it is a fleet manager's job. It is platform behaviour, and it is specified in decision records instead, which is also why it is the column where the hardest trade-offs live. Full reasoning in phasing.
