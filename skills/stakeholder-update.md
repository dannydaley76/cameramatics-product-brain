# Skill: Stakeholder Update

*Ported from my own working repo. Tailored: added the honesty rule about
metrics that aren't readable yet, which this capability will need in its first
quarter ([[../brain/registers/metrics]], [[../brain/registers/risks]] R-06).*

## When to use
Weekly or sprint-end update for founders, board or senior leadership. Readable
in 60 seconds, answerable without a follow-up meeting.

## Format

### Product update — [date]

**This sprint**
What shipped or moved meaningfully. Name the things, not the themes.

**Current focus**
What the team is heads-down on and why it matters.

**Coming next**
One or two things maximum.

**Numbers / signals**
Any metric worth calling out, by ID. A feedback theme. An experiment result.

**Decisions needed (if any)**
Explicit. Don't bury it.

## Conventions
- Lead with outcomes, not activity. "High-severity events triaged within 7 days
  hit 91% across live accounts" not "worked on the review queue".
- No jargon. If it needs explaining, rewrite it.
- 5–7 bullets total across all sections.
- If nothing material happened, say so. "Quiet sprint, focus on X" is a fine
  update and buys credibility for the ones that aren't.
- **Never report a metric as evidence before it can carry the weight.** For this
  capability, collision outcomes are not readable inside a quarter at
  single-fleet scale. Reporting an encouraging early collision number as a
  result is the fastest way to lose the right to report anything later. Say what
  it is: too early, here is the habit metric instead.
- Send it before people ask for it.
